# ADR-0037: Outcome reports deferred to FR-21

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-30

## Context
Opt-in outcome reports (FR-21) would feed confidence and evaluation, but their design touches consent and personal data.

## Decision
v0 stores no outcome reports. Their design is settled when FR-21 is picked up.

## Consequences
Confidence in v0 comes from sources and community stories only.
