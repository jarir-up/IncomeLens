# ADR-0009: Consultants and lawyers are later users

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-29

## Context
Visa consultants compile files for many clients, which needs multi-client workspaces.

## Decision
U2 (consultant or lawyer) is out of v0.

## Consequences
The local app assumes one applicant plus their sponsor.
