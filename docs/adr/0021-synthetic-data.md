# ADR-0021: Seeded synthetic evidence; personas by the owner

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-07 (IncomeLens ADR-012/023, carried forward)

## Context
Evaluation needs hidden ground truth.

## Decision
A seeded, deterministic generator driven by fact tables produces synthetic applicant evidence. A model may vary prose, never facts. The owner designs personas and scenarios through a questionnaire.

## Consequences
Applies to applicant evidence only (ADR-0003).
