# ADR-0001: Product scope: an evidence compiler, Schengen first

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-30

## Context
Earlier framings were either a benefits platform with no buildable v0 (Payoneer integration, money routing, front-paid claims) or a document Q&A system built to demonstrate skills. Neither produced an outcome a freelancer needs today.

## Decision
PakkiFile v0 is an evidence compiler for any opportunity that depends on proof of income and standing: a requirements registry, a local evidence vault, a mismatch engine and a readiness agent that exports a dossier. Schengen missions are the first authority class loaded. The benefits layer from the original concept is out of scope for this repo.

## Consequences
Needs no partner, licence or institution to recognise us on day one. Supersedes IncomeLens ADR-021 (coverage answer contract) and ADR-026 (contribution simulator); both belong to the benefits layer.
