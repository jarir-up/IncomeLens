# ADR-0011: Honesty boundary

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-29

## Context
Applicants act on what the product says, and some mismatches tempt concealment. Carries the advice boundary of IncomeLens ADR-020, approved 2026-10-07.

## Decision
PakkiFile never predicts approval or refusal, never gives legal, immigration or tax conclusions, never suggests hiding or working around a fact, and never alters evidence. It states risks truthfully and points to qualified advice. Confidence applies to requirements, never to outcomes.

## Consequences
Enforced by R-23 and R-29, plus an advice-boundary evaluation set.
