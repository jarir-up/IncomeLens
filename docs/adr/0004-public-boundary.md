# ADR-0004: Public product story, private strategy

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-10-29

## Context
The repo and the build-in-public story are the distribution channel. Governance, data-commons and commercial strategy are not ready to be public.

## Decision
The repo, product docs and posts are public. Governance, data-commons and commercial strategy live outside the repo.

## Consequences
Supersedes IncomeLens ADR-001, which kept product strategy out of the repo entirely.
